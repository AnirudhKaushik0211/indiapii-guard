IndiaPII Guard

Find and redact Aadhaar, PAN, UPI, phone, card and bank details before you paste text into an LLM. Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded or stored.

Help

What it does

IndiaPII Guard finds Indian personal identifiers in text, such as Aadhaar, PAN, UPI IDs, mobile, card and bank account numbers. It replaces them with tokens or masks, so you can paste the redacted text into an LLM prompt. Everything runs in this page; your text is never uploaded.

Quick start

  1. Paste or type your text into the Redact tab.
  2. Review the highlighted findings and the table.
  3. Untick a row to keep that value unredacted.
  4. Copy the redacted text and paste it into the LLM.

How confidence works

Each candidate starts with a base score from its structure and check digit. Context words nearby raise it, for example "aadhaar", "pan card", "mobile", "a/c", "khata number" or "call karna". Negative context such as "order", "otp" or "invoice" lowers it, because the number is probably a reference number. A finding is shown when its score reaches the threshold: Lenient (0.35), Balanced (0.50, the default) or Strict (0.70). The confidence is a rule-based score, not a probability.

Redaction styles

  • Token: 2345 6789 0124 (synthetic example) becomes [AADHAAR_1].
  • Mask: every letter and digit becomes X, so it becomes XXXX XXXX XXXX.
  • Partial: only the last 4 characters stay, so it becomes XXXX XXXX 0124.

Tokens are consistent within one text: the same value always gets the same token. They are not linkable across texts, because numbering starts again for each text. The token map, which links tokens to the original values, stays in this page.

Turning entities off

Unticking an identifier in Settings removes its findings after detection. A number that matched a disabled identifier is not passed on to another identifier, so it stays in the text unredacted.

Privacy

No network requests (blocked by a Content Security Policy and checked by automated tests). Nothing is uploaded or stored. The browser's localStorage holds only the "help seen" flag and, if you opt in, your settings, never your text. The clipboard and browser extensions are outside the page's control.

Limitations

  • Names and addresses are not detected.
  • Formats outside the rules above are not detected.
  • Numbers written in Devanagari digits are not detected.
  • Aadhaar VID, landline numbers and passport numbers are not detected in this version.
  • Aadhaar numbers that fail the checksum (mistyped numbers) are not flagged.
  • A 91-prefixed mobile number written without + or a space may be labelled as an Aadhaar number; it is still redacted.
  • A 12-digit bank account number that happens to pass the Aadhaar checksum (about 1 in 10 such numbers) may be labelled as an Aadhaar number when an Aadhaar label is nearby; it is still redacted.
  • The confidence is not a probability.
  • The published accuracy was measured on synthetic, self-constructed text.
